# Policies and support

## Late work

Work submitted after the deadline loses 5% of the available marks per day,
to a maximum of five days, after which it is not accepted. The midterm has
no late-work provision — it is sat in tutorial, under the conditions
described on its page, or not at all.

## Extensions

Ask your tutor before the deadline, not after. A short, specific reason is
enough; you do not need to disclose more than that. Extensions requested
after work is already late are considered case by case and are not
guaranteed.

## Academic integrity

The analysis must be yours. Discussing a case with a classmate is
encouraged — half the point of a market is that other people see things in
it you don't — but the signal design, the redesign, and the argument you
submit must be your own work, correctly cited where it draws on someone
else's. The work must be yours; the market failure isn't.

Every persona, profile, and case used across this course's assessments is
fictional. Submitting a real, identifiable person's real profile or
relationship — yours or anyone else's — is itself a breach, independent of
what mark it might earn.

## Getting help

Tutorials are the first place to ask — bring the specific thing you're
stuck on, not a general "I don't get it." Outside tutorial hours, contact
your tutor directly; contact details are on the [people](/people/) page.
