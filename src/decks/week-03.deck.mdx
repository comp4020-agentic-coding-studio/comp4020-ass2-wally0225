# Cheap Talk vs. Costly Signals

Why anyone can say "I'm loyal" — and why that's the whole problem

---

## Three ideas, before anything else

- cheap talk
- costly signal
- separating vs. pooling

Get these three straight now — the scenario in a minute uses all of them.

---

## Cheap talk

Saying "I'm looking for something real" costs nothing and risks nothing —
true or not, nobody pays for saying it.

Economists call this **cheap talk**: a claim that's free to make and free
to be wrong about.

---

## Costly signal

A half-marathon medal, photographed on an actual trail, is hard to fake
convincingly — you'd have to actually run the thing, or find someone who
did and borrow it.

That's a **costly signal**: informative *because* faking it would have
cost something.

---

## Separating vs. pooling

| Aspect | Separating | Pooling |
|---|---|---|
| What happens | different people send different signals | everyone sends the same signal, regardless |
| What you learn | something — the signal sorts people | nothing — it doesn't distinguish anyone |
| Dating example | only actual runners post race photos | everyone's bio says "I love to travel" |

---

## Now, back to the point

Two people. Same claim. Different evidence.

---

## Two profiles, same claim

**Jordan** and **Sam** both write: *"I love hiking."*

| Detail | Jordan | Sam |
|---|---|---|
| Claim | "I love hiking" | "I love hiking" |
| Evidence on profile | a marathon finisher photo, muddy boots, a trail-running watch in three other photos | one sunset photo, no other trace of it |
| Cost to have faked it | high — you'd need the race, the gear, the tan lines | close to zero — one photo, borrowed or staged |

---

## What that does to the claim

Jordan's claim **separates**: only someone who actually hikes ends up with
a race bib and gear in three unrelated photos. It's a costly signal.

Sam's claim **pools**: it's exactly what anyone would post, whether or not
it's true. It's cheap talk, dressed as detail.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> The claim was identical. The cost of making it wasn't. That's the whole
> theory.

## Tutorial 3

Ten fictional profile claims. Sort each into cheap talk or costly signal —

---

## What today doesn't settle

A costly signal earns your trust in the claim. It doesn't tell you the
claim was worth checking in the first place — Jordan's medal proves
discipline, not whether you'd get along on a Tuesday night.
