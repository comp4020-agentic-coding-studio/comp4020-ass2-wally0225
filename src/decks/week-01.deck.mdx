# Love, Markets, and Other Failures

A semester spent taking one metaphor seriously

---

## A market is

- a scarce thing
- allocated among people who want it
- under rules nobody fully controls

Housing is a market. University admission is a market.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

# This course's claim: courtship is one too

---

## What that buys us

- search costs — week 2
- signaling vs. cheap talk — week 3
- adverse selection — week 4
- screening — week 5
- optimal stopping — week 6

---

## What it costs us

The claim is **not** that romance is "just" economics.

It's narrower: wherever information is uneven and outcomes depend on what
others do, markets fail in specific, nameable ways.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> The semester is a tour of those failures, in order of how badly they
> hurt.

---

## The shape of the semester

| Act | Weeks | Question |
