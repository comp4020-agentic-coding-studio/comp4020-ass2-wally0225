# Screening Games

Designing a menu that makes the truth the easy choice

---

## Three ideas, before anything else

- screening
- self-selection
- incentive-compatible

Get these three straight now — the scenario in a minute uses all of them.

---

## Screening

Instead of asking "are you serious about this?" — a question anyone would
answer "yes" to — you build two different paths and watch which one people
actually take.

Economists call the side doing the asking-without-asking the
**screener**, and the act itself **screening**.

---

## Self-selection

Nobody is sorted by a judge. People sort themselves, each choosing whatever
path is actually best for their own situation — which is exactly what
makes the sorting trustworthy.

That's **self-selection**: the type reveals itself through the choice, not
through a claim.

---

## Incentive-compatible

A menu only works if the honest type genuinely prefers the option meant
for them, and the dishonest type genuinely doesn't want to copy it. If a
casual dater would happily pick the "serious" option too, the menu hasn't
separated anyone.

That property is **incentive compatibility** — and it's the whole game.

---

{/* _class: banner */}

## Now, back to the point

One app. Two tiers. See who picks which.

---

## Two tiers, one app

| Tier | Casual | Serious |
|---|---|---|
| Price | free | $30/month |
| To join | a few photos | a long compatibility questionnaire |
| Who this favors | someone not ready to invest time | someone ready to invest time |

---

## Does it actually separate?

| Question | Casual user | Serious user |
|---|---|---|
| Would they pay $30 for this? | no — not worth it to them | yes — worth it |
| Would they fill in the long form? | no — too much effort for a maybe | yes — they'd do it anyway |
| Which tier do they end up in? | Casual | Serious |

The menu works because the "cost" of the serious tier is exactly the thing
a casual user wouldn't pay and a serious one would.

---

## A screen that doesn't work

Compare a "Verified" badge that only requires a selfie matching your
profile photo. Anyone can take a selfie — casual and serious daters alike.

It costs nothing that correlates with actually being serious. It's a
**badge**, not a **screen**: it looks like screening but isn't
incentive-compatible.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> A menu that everyone would choose the same way isn't a screen. It's
> decoration.
