## Before the tutorial

Come with a strategy in mind: a rule for cooperate/defect against an
unknown opponent, played repeatedly, that you could state in one sentence.

## In the tutorial

Every strategy plays every other strategy across many rounds; totals are
tallied live. Simple, forgiving strategies tend to outperform aggressive or
overly clever ones — the point isn't to guess the winner in advance, it's
to see the folk theorem produce a result instead of just stating one.

## Afterwards

Whatever won, ask what made it exploitable or not, and what that implies
for how much a negotiated split (week 9) actually needs enforcement versus
how much it enforces itself.
