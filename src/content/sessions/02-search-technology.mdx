## Before the tutorial

Read week 2's lecture. Have a fictional persona in mind — reused from
tutorial 1 or invented fresh.

## In the tutorial

Working in pairs, pick one persona and sketch **two** different discovery
channels for them as cost functions: time per candidate, signal quality,
how often a promising lead turns out not to be one. Put the two side by
side and find the one number that's different enough to flip the optimal
strategy — screen harder before meeting, or say yes sooner. Swap with
another pair and check that your "flipping number" survives their
challenge.

## Afterwards

This is deliberately not "how to use dating apps better." The channel is a
parameter, not the point — the point is being able to say precisely which
number changed and which decision it changed.
