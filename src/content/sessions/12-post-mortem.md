## Before the tutorial

Look back over the semester's running example you've carried since
tutorial 1. No new material to read.

## In the tutorial

Group discussion, structured around two questions asked of everyone in
turn: what did the market framework explain well this semester, and what
did it visibly fail to explain? The second question is the harder one, and
credit goes to whoever can point at a specific failure rather than a vague
discomfort.

## Afterwards

This discussion is the direct rehearsal for the capstone's own required
act of self-doubt — bring what came up here into that final piece of work.
