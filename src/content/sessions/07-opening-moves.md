## Before the tutorial

Review week 7's lecture on initiation and bargaining. No written prep.

## In the tutorial

Warm-up drills first: short prompts in the midterm's own format — a photo,
a bio line, an offline scenario — worked through as a group with reasoning
made explicit, not just an answer. The midterm follows in the same session,
using the same format under exam conditions.

## Afterwards

Feedback on the midterm is returned the following week, alongside week 8's
material — by design, so you move straight from "what would you say" to
"what happens once you've said it and it worked."
