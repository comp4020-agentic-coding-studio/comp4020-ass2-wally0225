## Before the tutorial

Nothing to prepare — the personas and their partial information sheets are
handed out in the room.

## In the tutorial

Given a lot of fictional personas, each with a few visible attributes and
several hidden ones, choose who you'd pair with, then compare notes with
someone who saw a different subset of the same lot. The reveal at the end
shows why the average "car on the lot" looked worse than the average car in
the population — the mechanism, not just the moral.

## Afterwards

If you found yourself reasoning "the fact that they're still available is
itself information," you've derived adverse selection from the exercise
rather than being told about it — which is the point.
