## Before the tutorial

Bring a draft of the rule or mechanism your Assignment 2 redesign proposes,
however rough.

## In the tutorial

Swap drafts with a partner and try, deliberately, to find a way to game
each other's rule — a way a rational, self-interested participant could
follow it to the letter while defeating its intent. Revise your own rule in
response to what your partner found.

## Afterwards

A rule that survived being gamed once by a classmate is a much better bet
against a marker than one that's never been tested outside your own head.
