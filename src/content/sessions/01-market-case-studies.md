## Before the tutorial

Pick a market you've actually dealt with — not romance — and think about
where it got difficult. Not "it was annoying," but where specifically:
finding options, telling good ones from bad, agreeing on terms.

## In the tutorial

Small groups, each presenting one market for two minutes: what's being
allocated, who wants it, and where the friction sits. Costa (or whoever's
running the room) pushes for the specific mechanism, not the vibe.

## Afterwards

The market you picked becomes your running example for the next few weeks
— when a lecture introduces a new concept, you'll be asked whether it
shows up in your case, so the concept never stays abstract for long.
