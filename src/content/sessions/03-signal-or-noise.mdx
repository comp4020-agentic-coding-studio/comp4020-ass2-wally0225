## Before the tutorial

No preparation beyond the lecture — the batch of claims is supplied in the
room.

## In the tutorial

Given ten fictional profile claims, sort each into cheap talk or costly
signal, then compare sorts across the room. Disagreements are the point:
they usually turn on an unstated assumption about verification cost, and
naming that assumption is the actual skill.

## Afterwards

This sorting exercise is the direct precursor to Assignment 1, where you'll
build a profile and have to defend your own claims the same way you just
judged someone else's.
