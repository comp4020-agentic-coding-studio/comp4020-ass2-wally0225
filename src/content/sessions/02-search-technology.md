## Before the tutorial

Read week 2's lecture. Have a fictional persona in mind — reused from
tutorial 1 or invented fresh.

## In the tutorial

Working in pairs, sketch your persona's search technology as a rough cost
function: time per candidate, information quality, how often a promising
lead turns out not to be one. Swap with another pair and check whether
their model actually predicts a different strategy than yours.

## Afterwards

This is deliberately not "how to use dating apps better." The channel is a
parameter, not the point — the point is being able to say precisely what
changes when the parameter does.
