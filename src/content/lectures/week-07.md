Everything so far has assumed the hard part is finding and evaluating
candidates. It isn't — it's moving. Initiating reveals interest, and
revealed interest is information the other side can use against you, which
is exactly the structure of a war of attrition: both sides gain from
someone acting, both prefer the other side pays the cost, and the
equilibrium can be a standoff that makes both sides worse off than if
either had simply gone first.

This is the hinge week. Everything before it was about finding and reading
information; everything after it is about what happens once two sides
commit to act on what they've found. The midterm, sat this week, tests the
first half by giving you the moment itself and asking what you'd do with
it — not for a "right" opening line, but for a defensible read of the
situation.
