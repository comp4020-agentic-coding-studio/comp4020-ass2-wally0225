"I love hiking" costs nothing to say and nothing to be wrong about — cheap
talk. A half-marathon medal photographed on a real trail, on the other
hand, is costly to fake convincingly, which is exactly what makes it
informative. Signaling theory's whole content is this asymmetry: a signal
is only worth reading if it would have been expensive to send falsely.

The uncomfortable implication, which this week states outright rather than
leaves implicit, is that most of what a profile or a first date
communicates is cheap talk, and the skill this course is building is not
"write a better bio" but "tell the difference" — a skill that transfers
directly to Assignment 1.
