## The brief

> Build a fictional dating profile, then prove you know which parts of it
> are actually worth believing.

A profile is a small bundle of claims made under an incentive to look
good. This assignment asks you to build one deliberately — persona,
photo description, bio — and then step outside it and audit your own
work: for each claim, say whether it's cheap talk or a costly signal, and
what would have to be true for that classification to hold. Weak defenses
lose more marks than weak claims do.

The persona must be invented. No real person's real profile, including
your own, is submitted, referenced, or based closely enough on someone
identifiable to be recognized — the exercise is about signal design, not
about anyone's actual dating life.

## What you submit

A one-page fictional profile (persona name, a described photo, a short
bio) and a second page auditing it: each claim, its classification, and a
short defense. Evidence of your process (drafts, what you changed and why)
strengthens the second page but isn't required on its own.
