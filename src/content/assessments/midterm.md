## The brief

> Given the moment, say what you'd do — and say why that's a good move,
> not just a confident one.

Twenty items, each a fictional prompt: a described profile photo, a bio
line, or an offline scenario (a stranger's opening line at a party, a
comfortable silence that needs breaking). For each, you write what you'd
say and, in one sentence, the concept from weeks 1–7 that makes it a
reasonable move rather than a guess. A confident answer with no theory
behind it scores no better than a hesitant one with the right theory
attached.

## What you submit

Nothing beyond the sat paper — this is completed in tutorial 7, on paper
or on the class laptops, in the time allotted. No take-home component.

## How to prepare

A [six-item mock set](/assessments/midterm-prep/), one per week 2–7
concept, each with a model response that names the concept it's applying —
the same format as the real paper, worked through in the open.
